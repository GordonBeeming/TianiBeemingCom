A rectangular, multi-layered coffee sponge cake. This design consists of three layers of light sponge, each separated by intricate piping of rich coffee-flavored buttercream. The top is bordered with matching buttercream rosettes and generously decorated with a mix of milk, dark, and white chocolate spheres and shards of hand-made chocolate.
