A charming character birthday cake with a rich chocolate drip finish. This single-tier, round cake is covered in white fondant, providing a clean canvas for the thick, chocolate-colored drip. The design features a cute, minimalist character face, fondant cat-like ears, and a decorative pink bow. Colorful sprinkles accent the chocolate topping, which is crowned by a large fondant number '7'.
