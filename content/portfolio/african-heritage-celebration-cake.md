A striking three-tier wedding cake celebrating traditional Zulu heritage. The white fondant base tier features a braided border, while the royal blue middle tier is adorned with a draped golden sash and motifs inspired by traditional art and beadwork. The top tier is masterfully sculpted to resemble a clay pot (ukhamba), detailed with intricate, colorful piped patterns. The design is crowned with a plume of natural feathers, creating a meaningful centerpiece for a traditional wedding.
