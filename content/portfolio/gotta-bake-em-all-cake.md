A round celebration cake expertly decorated to resemble the character Pikachu. The entire cake is covered in vibrant yellow buttercream, applied in a detailed star-tip piping technique to create texture. The character's distinct features are crafted from fondant, including black-tipped ears, black and white eyes, a nose, mouth, and iconic red cheeks.
