A playful and friendly monster-themed cake, guaranteed to bring smiles to a birthday party. The main cake is covered in a fun, shaggy orange buttercream 'fur' and brought to life with big, expressive fondant eyes, a cheerful smile, and striped horns. It's accompanied by a little three-eyed monster friend, hand-sculpted from smooth fondant, making this duo a fantastic centerpiece for a fun-loving child's celebration.
