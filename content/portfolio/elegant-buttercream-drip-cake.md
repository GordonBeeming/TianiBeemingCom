An elegant, single-tier drip cake with a soft color palette. The sides feature a delicate watercolor effect in pink and white buttercream with a clean white chocolate ganache drip. A border of chocolate sprinkles trims the base. The top holds a pool of smooth fruit curd, encircled by meticulously piped pink and white buttercream rosettes lightly decorated with chocolate sprinkles.
