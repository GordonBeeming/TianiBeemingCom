A bright and cheerful two-tier celebration cake with a rustic yet elegant sunflower theme. Both tiers are frosted in smooth white buttercream and feature a luscious, golden caramel drip. The cake is beautifully adorned with large, fresh sunflowers, complemented by delicate white roses and sprays of baby's breath.
