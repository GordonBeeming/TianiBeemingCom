A heart-shaped cake entirely covered in delicate, hand-piped white buttercream rosettes. This thoughtful design is decorated with several handcrafted fondant elements: a pink awareness ribbon, a pencil, and a personal note that reads, 'To good times and through bad times.' It's a heartfelt way to show appreciation, support, or to celebrate a milestone for healthcare professionals.
