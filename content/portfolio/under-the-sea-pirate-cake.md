A two-tier, pirate-themed birthday cake. The bottom tier is covered in dark blue fondant and features a white anchor, edible seaweed details, and a crumbled cookie 'sand' base. The top tier is decorated with light blue and white marbled buttercream to simulate waves, and includes sculpted green fondant tentacles, a small treasure chest, and a pink mermaid tail. The cake is finished with black, tattered sails on wooden dowels.
