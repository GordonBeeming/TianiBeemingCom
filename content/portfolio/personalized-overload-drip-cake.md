A vibrant and fully loaded drip cake, perfect for a fun-filled boy's birthday. This tall, single-tier cake is frosted in bright blue buttercream and features a contrasting, glossy red drip. The top is piled high with an assortment of treats, including French macarons, Ferrero Rocher chocolates, wafer sticks, colorful candies, and a fondant soccer ball. The cake is finished with a custom name and age topper.
