An elegant and modern drip cake, perfect for a stylish teen's milestone birthday. The cake features a beautiful watercolor buttercream effect in shades of teal and white, with a creamy white chocolate ganache drip. It is adorned with handcrafted white sugar roses with delicate gold-dusted edges, a bold number '14' topper, and finished with scattered gold and white pearl accents for a touch of sparkle.
