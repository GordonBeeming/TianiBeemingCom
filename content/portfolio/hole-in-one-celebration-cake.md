A round celebration cake themed as a personal golf green, perfect for any golf enthusiast. The top features a smooth green fondant putting surface with a custom 'Happy Birthday' message, a hole with a red flag, and a single golf ball ready to be sunk. The design includes a realistic sand trap made from crumbled cookie and is bordered by detailed green buttercream grass piping that continues down the sides of the cake.
