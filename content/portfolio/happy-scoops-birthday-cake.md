A tall, 3D sculpted cake designed to look like a triple-scoop ice cream cone. The structure stands on a realistic, textured fondant waffle cone base. It features three large, stacked scoops of 'ice cream'—in white, purple, and pink—each decorated with a unique and friendly fondant character face. The top scoop is finished with a sweet pink drizzle, a topping of colourful sprinkles, and a classic red fondant cherry.
