A custom-sculpted cake designed to look like a realistic vintage suitcase. The entire cake is covered in tan-colored fondant, featuring meticulously crafted details like stitched leather-effect straps, metallic-dusted buckles, corner protectors, and a central handle. A red fondant flag is tucked under a strap for a pop of color, making this design perfect for a farewell or bon voyage celebration.
