A unique three-tier wedding cake that combines multiple popular styles in one cohesive design. The bottom tier features smooth white fondant, the middle tier is a rich chocolate drip cake, and the top tier showcases a rustic semi-naked finish. A lush cascade of fresh white roses and delicate sprays of blue baby's breath flows down the side, and the cake is completed with an elegant gold 'Mr & Mrs' topper.
