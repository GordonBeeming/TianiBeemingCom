An adorable novelty birthday cake designed to look like a charming gift box full of kittens. The cake is covered in light pink fondant with white polka dots. A propped-open lid, topped with a large, elegant white fondant bow, creates the gift box effect. Peeking out from inside are three hand-sculpted fondant kittens—ginger, white, and tabby—each with sweet, detailed faces.
