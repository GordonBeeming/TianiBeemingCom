A round celebration cake with a light blue fondant base, creating a sky scene. The cake features a prominent, handcrafted fondant rainbow arching across the top, complete with fluffy white fondant clouds at its base. A whimsical, hand-sculpted unicorn figure with a pink mane and golden horn stands on a patch of green fondant grass, appearing to 'eat' a colorful assortment of sprinkles spilling from the rainbow. The design is finished with white cloud and green grass details around the base.
