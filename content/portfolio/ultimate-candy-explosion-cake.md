A vibrant and playful drip cake featuring a pastel rainbow and an explosion of sweet treats. The sides are decorated with soft, horizontal stripes of rainbow-colored buttercream and topped with a creamy white chocolate drip. The cake is lavishly decorated with a variety of confections, including a giant lollipop, a handcrafted fondant rainbow, meringue kisses, cookies, and an assortment of colorful candies.
