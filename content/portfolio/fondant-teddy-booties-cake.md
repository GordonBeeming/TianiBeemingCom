A two-tier celebration cake designed for a baby shower. Both tiers are covered in smooth white fondant and wrapped with a delicate pink ribbon accent. The cake is adorned with a hand-sculpted pink fondant teddy bear, a matching baby bottle, and scattered pink and white fondant flowers. The design is finished with a textured ruffle border and topped with a pair of handcrafted pink baby booties.
