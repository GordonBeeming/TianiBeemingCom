A simple and sweet celebration cake with a vibrant two-tone color scheme. The cake features striped blue and purple buttercream around the base, with a clean white buttercream finish on the upper section. A decorative border of alternating blue and purple piped rosettes crowns the top of the cake, making it perfect for birthdays where a splash of color is needed.
