A creative and playful tall cake designed to look just like a juicy slice of watermelon. The buttercream exterior features striped green and white rind details at the base, with the main body in a vibrant pink. Teardrop-shaped black fondant 'seeds' are dotted around the sides, and the design is finished with a 'Happy Birthday' message on top.
