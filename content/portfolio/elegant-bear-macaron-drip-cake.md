An elegant drip cake with a gentle pastel blue theme, perfect for a baby shower. This tall, single-tier cake is frosted in smooth light blue buttercream and features a creamy white chocolate drip. The top is beautifully decorated with a central, hand-made teddy bear figure, fresh white roses, artistic white chocolate shards, and delicate blue and white meringue kisses. Light blue French macarons are placed on top and at the base to complete the design.
