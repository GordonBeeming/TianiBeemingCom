A whimsical and magical celebration cake, perfect for a unicorn-themed birthday party. The design features a hand-sculpted unicorn figure sitting atop the cake amongst fluffy fondant clouds. A large, colorful pastel rainbow wraps around the front, while a bouquet of pastel 'balloons' creates a festive backdrop. The cake is finished with golden star accents for a touch of magic.
