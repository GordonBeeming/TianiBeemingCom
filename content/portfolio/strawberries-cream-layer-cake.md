An elegant, rectangular layer cake piled high with fresh strawberries and chocolate. This celebration slice features two thick layers of light vanilla sponge, separated by a generous layer of smooth vanilla cream. The top is bordered with matching piped cream rosettes. The center is lavishly decorated with a mix of fresh, juicy strawberries, decadent chocolate-dipped strawberries, and artistic shards of both white and dark chocolate.
