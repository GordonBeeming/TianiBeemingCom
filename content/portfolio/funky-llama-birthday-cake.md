A fun and funky novelty cake designed to look like a friendly llama. The cake is covered in textured white buttercream to create a 'fur' effect and features a sweet, hand-modelled fondant face with long eyelashes. The llama is decorated with a colorful, fiesta-style blanket and pom-poms made from fondant, and is finished with a custom name and age topper.
