A charming, tall birthday cake designed for a young dog lover. This single-tier cake features a beautiful blue ombré buttercream finish, with black fondant paw prints climbing up the sides. The top creates a playful scene with several different breeds of hand-sculpted fondant dogs, complete with cute details like a bone and a food bowl. The design is finished with a personalized, glittery name topper.
