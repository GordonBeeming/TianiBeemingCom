A two-tier, semi-naked wedding cake combining rustic charm with modern elegance. Each tier features a light coating of buttercream and a rich, dark green ganache drip. The cake is lavishly decorated with fresh cream-colored roses, complementary greenery, and delicate filler flowers. Touches of edible gold paint on select leaves and buds add a hint of glamour to the design.
