A fun and muddy chocolate birthday cake, perfect for an adventurous boy who loves creepy crawlies. The cake is frosted with a rustic chocolate buttercream and topped with a generous layer of crushed chocolate cookie 'dirt.' It's brought to life with hand-sculpted fondant creatures, including a large, detailed brown spider and a cute green snake, all nestled amongst lush fondant leaves and personalized with the birthday boy's name.
