The ultimate celebration cake for any true chocolate aficionado. This rich, single-tier cake is frosted with a decadent chocolate buttercream and generously topped with a wreath of assorted chocolates and treats. The decorations include Lindt Lindor truffles, Oreo cookies, malted chocolate balls, honeycomb pieces, and colorful chocolate candies.
