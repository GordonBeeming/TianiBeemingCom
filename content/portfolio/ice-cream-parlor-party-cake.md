A cheerful celebration cake decorated with a festival of colorful buttercream piping. The smooth, white buttercream base provides the perfect canvas for the vibrant decorations. The top is completely covered in a dense mosaic of hand-piped rosettes and stars, with a matching border around the base, using a fun, multi-color palette of pink, blue, yellow, purple, and green.
