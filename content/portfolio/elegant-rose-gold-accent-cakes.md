A pair of elegant, individual-sized wedding cakes designed for a sophisticated celebration. Each cake is covered in white fondant featuring a classic quilted pattern with delicate gold dot accents. A simple white ribbon is tied around the circumference, and the design is finished with a topping of fresh, cream-colored roses. This style is perfect for intimate weddings or as a feature for the main table.
