An elegant two-tier cake featuring a romantic pink and gold color palette. The cake is frosted in a soft, pink watercolor-style buttercream, with a stunning cascade of edible gold leaf flowing down the side for a touch of luxury. The design is finished with an arrangement of fresh pink and white roses, delicate dried flowers, and both pink and gold-painted French macarons.
