A magical unicorn-themed birthday cake. This single-tier cake is covered in smooth white buttercream and decorated with a whimsical swirl of rainbow-colored buttercream 'hair' cascading down the top and sides. It features a tall, elegant golden fondant horn and delicate white fondant ears with gold-painted inner details. The unicorn's sweet face is completed with simple black piped eyelashes.
