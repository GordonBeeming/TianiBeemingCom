A proud three-tier wedding cake celebrating traditional South African heritage. The white fondant base is adorned with a striking geometric pattern inspired by traditional beadwork. The middle tier is elegantly wrapped in blue satin ribbons. The cake is crowned with a hand-sculpted black potjie pot—a symbol of community and celebration—and the entire design is unified with deep blue roses and accented with delicate feathers.
